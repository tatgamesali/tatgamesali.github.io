Privacy Policy

For mobile games published by TAT Games

Last updated: November 17, 2026

TAT Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) publishes mobile games, including games available on Apple platforms (collectively, the “Games”). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use our Games. Features and third-party services may vary between individual Games.

Publisher review required: Before publishing, verify the descriptions of data collected, ad personalization, consent, retention/deletion and children's audiences against your actual app builds and SDK settings. This notice should be removed after verification.

1. Information Processed

Depending on the Game and services enabled in it, our advertising and analytics providers may process:

  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system and version, app version, language, and technical diagnostics.
  • Identifiers: app-instance or device identifiers and, where permitted and available, an advertising identifier.
  • Gameplay and usage information: game sessions, progress events, interactions, and performance data.
  • Advertising information: ad impressions, ad interactions, and information used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Approximate location: a general region that may be inferred from IP address where processed by a provider.

The particular categories processed depend on the specific Game, the integrated SDK versions, user choices, device settings, and providers' current practices. We do not intend this list to mean that every Game collects every category.

2. Why Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate and improve our Games; measure performance and gameplay; display, deliver and measure advertisements; detect abuse and advertising fraud; diagnose technical issues; and comply with applicable legal obligations. Advertising may be contextual or personalized, depending on applicable consent, platform permissions, and configuration.

4. Advertising Choices and Tracking

On Apple devices, access to the advertising identifier for tracking is subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency requirements. Where applicable, the Game will request permission before tracking. You can review device-level privacy controls in iOS Settings. Declining tracking permission does not necessarily remove advertisements.

In jurisdictions requiring additional consent, relevant advertising and analytics processing must follow applicable consent and privacy requirements. Publisher check: confirm your games' actual consent-management implementation before publication.
